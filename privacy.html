Privacy

Your chat history is stored only in your own browser (localStorage). We do not run a database and do not collect accounts or personal data.

Messages you send are forwarded to a third-party AI provider (Google Gemini or Groq) to generate answers, and are subject to that provider's policies. Do not share sensitive information. The server may briefly see your IP address for rate limiting.

We keep only anonymous daily totals (number of visits, questions and errors). They contain no message text, names or IP addresses.
